What buyers will pay for

  • Added value Bathroom converted to a full bath. The 2019 layout had two half baths sharing a shower room, which buyers and appraisers read as a weakness. It now counts as 2.5 baths, more than most comps.
  • Added value New electrical panel, a key item for insurers on a 1959 home.
  • Added value Granite kitchen with island and stainless appliances, walk-in primary closet.
  • Added value Wired concrete block workshop shed, covered rear porch, large backyard.
  • Added value Flood zone X. Three of the closest comps (141 Alhambra, 117 Dahlia, 133 Dahlia Ct) sit in AE, where buyers face flood insurance.
  • Added value A real garage. Nearly every Lake Harriet comp has a carport or nothing.

Confirm before listing

  • Verify Roof and A/C age. The two strongest comps list a 2022 roof; if ours is older, expect buyers to price it in.
  • Verify Permits for the bath conversion and panel. Lenders, insurers and appraisers will ask.
  • Verify Update public records and MLS to 2.5 baths. Records still show 1 full and 1 half.
  • Verify Consider a septic pump and inspection report. 163 Lake Harriet did this and marketed the clear result.

Recommendation

List at $314,900, targeting a sale between $305,000 and $312,000 and a contract within 14 to 30 days. The home is the only updated 3-bedroom in Lake Harriet Estates coming to market, and once the two pendings close, buyers in the $300,000 range will have nothing else like it with a garage and flood zone X.

Since the 2019 purchase at $199,000, the estimated value of $312,500 is a gain of about 57%.

Marketing focus

  • Lead with the 2.5 baths, new electrical panel and flood zone X. These separate it from the AE comps and the older 1959 homes.
  • Photograph the workshop shed and backyard. Few homes at this price offer that space.
  • Have roof age, A/C age, permits and a wind mitigation report ready at launch. Insurance cost is a deciding factor for buyers of 1950s homes.
  • Accept FHA, VA and conventional offers. Two of the four key comps closed with FHA or conventional financing.
